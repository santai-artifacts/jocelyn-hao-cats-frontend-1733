Our Story

A cozy home for cats who need one

Whisker Haven began in 2018 when founder Emma Clarke fostered her first litter of kittens and never really stopped. What started in a spare bedroom grew into a full rescue dedicated to finding warm, loving homes for cats of all ages.

We're a small, volunteer-run shelter based in Portland, OR. Every cat in our care lives in a real foster home — never a cage — so they arrive socialized, confident, and ready for yours.

  • 🏡Foster-based care — our cats live in homes, not kennels
  • 💉All cats are vaccinated, spayed/neutered & microchipped
  • 🤝Lifetime support — we're here long after adoption day
  • 🌿No-kill, values-first rescue since 2018

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about adopting from Whisker Haven.

How long does the adoption process take? +

Most adoptions complete within one to two weeks from application to homecoming. After submitting your form, we review it within 2–3 business days, schedule a meet-and-greet, and do a quick home check. We move at a pace comfortable for both you and the cat.

What is the adoption fee? +

Our fee is $125 for adult cats (1+ years) and $175 for kittens under 1 year. Senior cats (7+) are $75. Fees cover spay/neuter, microchipping, all vaccinations, a flea treatment, and a starter pack with food and essentials to help your new cat settle in.

Can I meet the cat before committing? +

Absolutely — we encourage it! After your application is approved, we'll arrange a meet-and-greet at the foster home. You can spend time with the cat, ask the foster family questions, and get a genuine feel for the cat's personality before making any decision.

Do you adopt outside of Portland? +

We adopt within a 100-mile radius of Portland, OR. Out-of-area applications are considered case by case, especially for cats who've struggled to find a local match. Contact us to discuss your situation — we're flexible for the right fit.

Are cats good with kids or other animals? +

Each cat's profile includes notes on compatibility with children, dogs, and other cats — based on real observations from their foster stay, not guesses. Tell us about your household in the application and we'll help match you with a cat who'll thrive there.

What support do you offer after adoption? +

We're here for the lifetime of your cat. You'll have direct access to your foster family for the first 30 days, and our team is reachable by email any time for behavior questions, vet recommendations, or to share a great photo (we love those). You're never on your own after adoption day.
